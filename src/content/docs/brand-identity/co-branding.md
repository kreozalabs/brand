## Logo Lockup Rules

When pairing the Kreoza logo alongside a partner, community plugin, or integration logo, follow these core visual guidelines:

### 1. Visual Balance & Sizing

- **Equal Optical Weight:** Logos should appear equal in visual mass (adjusting for height vs. width, not strictly exact pixel bounding boxes).
- **Alignment:** Keep logos **optically centered** along the horizontal alignment line.
- **Clear Space:** Maintain a clear margin around the lockup equal to at least **25%** of the logo's height.

### 2. Lockup Structure

Use a simple neutral divider (`+`, `×`, or `|`) with equal spacing on both sides:

```
[ Your Logo ]   +   [ Kreoza Logo ]
```

### Quick Do's & Don'ts

| ✅ DO                                         | ❌ DON'T                                         |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- |
| Use official, unmodified logo SVG files.      | Stretch, distort, rotate, or recolor the logos.  |
| Maintain clean separation and clear space.    | Merge logos into a single hybrid mark or icon.   |
| Use dark/light mode compatible logo variants. | Place low-contrast logos on complex backgrounds. |

## Naming & Trademarks

When naming your integration, plugin, or community event, make sure the distinction between your product and official Kreoza software is crystal clear.

### Official Name vs. Technical Handles

- **Official Brand Name:** Always refer to the brand as **Kreoza** in all titles, copy, community graphics, and documentation. Avoid referring to the brand as "Kreoza Labs" or "kreozalabs".
- **Technical Handles:** `kreozalabs` is used strictly as an ecosystem handle and namespace on technical platforms (e.g. GitHub `github.com/kreozalabs` and NPM scope `@kreozalabs/*`).

### Approved Naming Patterns

| Context                   | Approved Pattern ✅                 | Prohibited Pattern ❌                      |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| **Plugins / Extensions**  | _[Name] plugin for Kreoza_          | _Kreoza [Name]_                            |
| **Events & Meetups**      | _[Community Name] featuring Kreoza_ | _Kreoza & Cocktails_ / _Kreoza Conference_ |
| **Forks & Distributions** | _[Name], built on Kreoza Core_      | _Kreoza Community Edition_                 |

:::caution[Written Approval Required]
Printing logos on physical merchandise (t-shirts, stickers, swag) or claiming official co-hosting/partnerships requires prior written permission from Kreoza.

:::

## FAQ

### Social Media & Graphics

- **Can I use the Kreoza logo in a social graphic?**  
  **Yes** — as long as it's to announce an approved partnership, integration, or speaking session. Do not present your project as an official Kreoza launch.

### Event Titles & Presentations

- **Can we say we're "Partnering with Kreoza" or "Co-hosting"?**  
  **No** — unless you have explicit written approval. Use phrasing like _"Community meetup featuring Kreoza"_.
- **Can I use Kreoza product names or mascots in an event title?**  
  **No** — product names and trademarks cannot be used directly in event names (e.g., avoid _"Kreoza & Cocktails"_).
- **Can I include the logo on presentation slides?**  
  **Yes** — if speaking as part of an official program. Always observe clear-space and size rules.

### Websites & Swag

- **Can I create a landing page about our integration?**  
  **Yes** — you may display the Kreoza logo to indicate compatibility, but it should not be the primary hero branding element.
- **Can I print the logo on t-shirts, swag, or stickers?**  
  **No** — printing logos on physical merchandise requires prior written approval from Kreoza.
